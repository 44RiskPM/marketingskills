# 44RiskPM Marketing Strategy

Marketing ideas for **44RiskPM** — exam prep courses for PMP and PMI-RMP certifications, targeting busy professionals looking to get certified.

**Current state:** Growth is flat with occasional spikes. Marketing is social media only.

---

## Diagnosis: Why Growth Is Flat

Flat growth with spikes is the classic pattern of a business running on **rented channels only**. Social media gives you spikes when a post hits, but no compounding engine underneath. The fix is building **owned channels** (email list, SEO content, community) that grow steadily while social amplifies them.

The three biggest gaps right now:

1. **No search engine presence** — People actively Google "PMP exam prep," "PMI-RMP study guide," "how to pass the PMP" every day. Without content targeting these searches, you're invisible to high-intent buyers.
2. **No email list** — Social followers aren't yours. An email list converts 3-5x better than social and doesn't depend on algorithms.
3. **No referral loop** — Every student who passes their exam is a walking testimonial. There's no system to turn that moment into new students.

---

## Top 10 Marketing Ideas (Prioritized)

### 1. SEO Content Engine (High Impact, Free)

**What:** Create blog content targeting searches your audience already makes.

**Why it fits:** Certification candidates are active searchers. They Google study tips, exam breakdowns, concept explanations, and comparisons. This is the highest-ROI channel for an education business.

**How to start:**
- Write 5 foundational articles: "How to Pass the PMP Exam," "PMP vs PMI-RMP: Which Certification First," "PMI-RMP Exam Study Plan for Busy Professionals," "PMP Exam Format & What to Expect," "How Long Does It Take to Study for the PMP"
- Target long-tail keywords like "PMP exam tips for working professionals" and "PMI-RMP exam difficulty"
- Publish one article per week, minimum

**Expected outcome:** Organic traffic within 3-6 months that compounds. Each article is a permanent lead generation asset.

**Resources needed:** 3-5 hours/week writing (or repurpose your existing course content into articles)

---

### 2. Email List + Lead Magnet (High Impact, Low Cost)

**What:** Build an email list using a high-value free resource as the hook.

**Why it fits:** Busy professionals don't impulse-buy a certification course. They research, compare, and decide over days or weeks. Email lets you stay in front of them during that decision window.

**Lead magnet ideas:**
- **"PMP Exam Readiness Checklist"** — A downloadable checklist of everything they need before sitting the exam
- **"7-Day PMP Study Kickstart Plan"** — A mini email course that gives them a taste of your teaching style
- **"50 PMP Exam Questions You'll Probably See"** — Practice questions that demonstrate your expertise

**How to start:**
1. Create one lead magnet (the 7-day email course is best — it showcases your teaching)
2. Add email capture to your website and social profiles
3. Build a 6-email welcome sequence: deliver value → build trust → present your course

**Expected outcome:** Convert 20-40% of website visitors into email subscribers. Email converts to course sales at 2-5%, far higher than social.

**Resources needed:** One weekend to create the lead magnet. Use a tool like Kit (ConvertKit), Mailchimp, or Resend for automation.

---

### 3. Free Tool: PMP/RMP Exam Readiness Quiz (High Impact, Medium Effort)

**What:** Build an interactive quiz that tells visitors if they're ready for the PMP or PMI-RMP exam.

**Why it fits:** Certification candidates are anxious about readiness. A quiz scratches that itch while qualifying leads. People who score low need your course. People who score high still want validation.

**How to start:**
1. Create a 15-20 question quiz covering key knowledge areas
2. Gate the results behind an email capture ("Get your personalized study plan")
3. Segment results: "Ready to sit the exam" vs. "Need more prep" → tailor follow-up emails accordingly
4. Build with Typeform, Tally, or Outgrow (no code needed)

**Expected outcome:** Quizzes convert at 30-50% for email capture. This becomes your top lead generation asset.

**Resources needed:** 1-2 days to build. You already have the expertise to write the questions.

---

### 4. YouTube Channel (High Impact, Medium Effort)

**What:** Create YouTube videos teaching PM concepts, exam tips, and study strategies.

**Why it fits:** "PMP exam prep" has massive YouTube search volume. Video is the #1 format for education content. Busy professionals watch study videos during commutes, lunch breaks, and evenings.

**How to start:**
- Record 5 videos: "PMP Exam in 2026: Everything You Need to Know," "5 PMP Concepts Most People Get Wrong," "My PMI-RMP Study Plan," "PMP vs PMI-RMP — Which Should You Get First," "How I'd Pass the PMP in 30 Days"
- Keep videos 8-15 minutes. Talking head + slides works fine.
- End every video with a CTA to your lead magnet or course

**Expected outcome:** YouTube videos rank in both YouTube and Google search. Compounds over time. One strong video can drive leads for years.

**Resources needed:** 3-4 hours per video (recording + basic editing). Phone camera quality is fine to start.

---

### 5. Student Referral Program (High Impact, Low Cost)

**What:** Give students who pass their exam a reason and a mechanism to refer colleagues.

**Why it fits:** Passing a certification exam is an emotional high. People celebrate on LinkedIn, tell coworkers, and get asked "how did you prepare?" This is the highest-trust marketing channel — word of mouth from someone who succeeded.

**How to start:**
1. Create a simple referral offer: "Give a friend 15% off, get a $50 Amazon gift card when they enroll"
2. Trigger the referral ask at the moment of success: right after they report passing
3. Provide shareable assets: a pre-written LinkedIn post template, a referral link
4. Follow up at 7 and 30 days if they haven't shared yet

**Expected outcome:** 10-20% of successful students refer at least one person. Referred students have higher LTV and lower churn.

**Resources needed:** A referral tracking link (even a simple UTM works to start) and an email sequence.

---

### 6. LinkedIn Authority Building (Medium Impact, Free)

**What:** Shift your social strategy from generic posting to structured thought leadership on LinkedIn specifically.

**Why it fits:** Your audience — working professionals seeking career advancement — lives on LinkedIn. LinkedIn's algorithm currently favors educational content and personal stories.

**Content pillars to post about:**
- **Exam tips** — Bite-sized study advice (e.g., "The #1 mistake PMP candidates make on risk questions")
- **Student wins** — Celebrate when students pass (with permission). Social proof is your best ad.
- **Career ROI** — Share data on salary increases after PMP/RMP certification
- **Risk management insights** — Position yourself as the go-to voice for project risk (your unique angle)
- **Behind-the-scenes** — How you build courses, what students struggle with, your teaching philosophy

**How to start:**
- Post 3-5 times per week on LinkedIn
- Every post ends with a soft CTA (link to lead magnet, quiz, or course)
- Engage in PM-related comments and groups daily for 15 minutes

**Expected outcome:** Build a targeted following of PM professionals. LinkedIn posts drive direct course inquiries.

---

### 7. Podcast Guest Tour (Medium Impact, Free)

**What:** Guest on project management, career development, and professional certification podcasts.

**Why it fits:** There are dozens of PM and career-focused podcasts looking for expert guests. One 30-minute interview puts you in front of a warm, engaged audience. Busy professionals consume podcasts during commutes.

**How to start:**
1. List 20 podcasts in the PM, career development, and professional growth space
2. Pitch yourself with a specific topic: "How Busy Professionals Can Pass the PMP in 90 Days" or "Why Risk Management Is the Most Underrated PM Skill"
3. Prepare a compelling personal story (why you teach this, a student transformation story)
4. Mention your lead magnet in every episode

**Expected outcome:** Each podcast appearance drives 20-100+ email signups. Builds authority and backlinks.

---

### 8. Webinars + Live Study Sessions (Medium Impact, Low Cost)

**What:** Host free live webinars on exam-relevant topics as lead generation events.

**Why it fits:** Webinars let prospects experience your teaching before buying. For exam prep, a live "study session" format is especially compelling — it reduces the risk of buying a course they might not like.

**Webinar topic ideas:**
- "PMP Exam Crash Course: The 5 Domains in 60 Minutes"
- "Live Q&A: Your PMP Exam Questions Answered"
- "PMI-RMP Deep Dive: Risk Management Frameworks"
- "Study Planning Workshop for Working Professionals"

**How to start:**
1. Host one webinar per month
2. Promote on social media and to your email list
3. Record and repurpose as YouTube content + blog posts
4. Make an offer at the end (limited-time discount on your course)

**Expected outcome:** 100-300 registrants per webinar, 30-50% attend live. 5-10% of attendees convert to paid.

---

### 9. Glossary / Knowledge Base SEO (Medium Impact, Low Effort)

**What:** Create a "PM Certification Glossary" with a page for every key term — EVM, WBS, Risk Register, Monte Carlo Simulation, etc.

**Why it fits:** Students Google these terms constantly while studying. Each glossary page targets a "what is [term]" search query and funnels visitors toward your course.

**How to start:**
- List 50-100 key terms from PMP and PMI-RMP exam content
- Write a clear, 300-500 word explanation for each
- Link each term to relevant course modules
- Publish as `/glossary/[term]` pages

**Expected outcome:** Hundreds of long-tail keyword rankings. Each page is a micro-funnel into your email list or course.

**Resources needed:** You already know these terms — the content is in your head. Batch-write 5-10 per sitting.

---

### 10. Comparison & Alternative Pages (Medium Impact, Low Effort)

**What:** Create pages comparing 44RiskPM to competitors and alternatives.

**Why it fits:** People searching "best PMP exam prep courses" or "[competitor] vs [competitor]" are at the decision stage — they already want to buy, they're just deciding from whom.

**Pages to create:**
- "Best PMP Exam Prep Courses in 2026 (Compared)"
- "44RiskPM vs [Top Competitor]: Which PMP Course Is Right for You"
- "PMI-RMP Exam Prep: Your Options Compared"
- "Affordable PMP Exam Prep for Busy Professionals"

**How to start:**
1. Research your top 3-5 competitors
2. Write honest, detailed comparisons highlighting your strengths (personalized coaching, focus on risk management, designed for busy professionals)
3. Target keywords like "best PMP prep course," "[competitor] alternative," "[competitor] review"

**Expected outcome:** Capture high-intent buyers actively comparing options.

## Content Repurposing Flywheel

Every piece of content should become 5+ pieces:

```
Blog Article
├── LinkedIn post (key takeaway)
├── YouTube video (teach the same topic)
├── Email to list (summary + link)
├── Instagram/TikTok short (one tip from the article)
└── Podcast talking point (discuss on guest appearances)
```

This is how you turn flat-line growth into compounding growth. One hour of effort multiplied across channels.
