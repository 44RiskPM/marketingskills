Marketing ideas for **44RiskPM** — exam prep courses for PMP and PMI-RMP certifications, targeting busy professionals looking to get certified.

| Channel | Status |
|---------|--------|

| Substack | Active newsletter |

**Current state:** Growth is flat with occasional spikes despite having multiple channels running.

---

## Revised Diagnosis: Why Growth Is Flat

You don't have a channel problem — you have a **connection problem**. The pieces exist (YouTube, blog, email, Substack, social) but they're likely operating as separate islands rather than a single growth system. This creates the flat-with-spikes pattern: each channel generates occasional wins but nothing compounds.

**The three real issues:**

### 1. Your channels aren't feeding each other
50+ YouTube videos should be driving email signups. 153 Kit subscribers suggests the pipeline from video viewer → email subscriber is broken or missing. Every video needs a reason to join your list. Every blog post needs an email capture. Your Substack and Kit list may be splitting your audience instead of combining forces.

### 2. No high-converting lead magnet
153 subscribers after 50+ videos means the "subscribe to my newsletter" pitch isn't compelling enough. You need a specific, irresistible free resource that certification candidates can't say no to — something worth trading their email for.

### 3. No system to convert attention into revenue
Views and followers are vanity metrics without a path to purchase. The question isn't "how do I get more eyeballs" — it's "what happens after someone watches a video or reads a post?"

---

## The Growth System (How to Connect Everything)

```
YouTube Video / Blog Post / Social Post
        │
        ▼
  Lead Magnet (quiz, checklist, mini-course)
        │
        ▼
  Email List (Kit — consolidate here)
        │
        ▼
  Nurture Sequence (value → trust → offer)
        │
        ▼
  Course Sale
        │
        ▼
  Student Success → Referral Loop
```

Every piece of content you create should push people one step down this funnel. Right now, your content likely dead-ends — someone watches a video, enjoys it, and leaves. The system above captures them and moves them toward a purchase.

## Top 10 Actions (Prioritized for Your Situation)

### 1. Consolidate Email: Pick Kit OR Substack (Not Both)

**The problem:** Running both Kit and Substack splits your audience, your effort, and your data. 153 subscribers in Kit + whatever's on Substack = a fragmented list that's hard to nurture.

**The fix:** Pick one and go all-in.

| Factor | Kit | Substack |
|--------|-----|----------|
| Automation sequences | Yes (strong) | No |
| Tagging & segmentation | Yes | No |
| Built-in discovery | No | Yes (Substack network) |
| Landing pages | Yes | Limited |
| Course-seller integration | Better | Worse |

**Recommendation: Kit.** You're selling courses, not running a media company. You need automation (welcome sequences, segmented nurture, launch emails). Kit does this. Substack doesn't.

**Action steps:**
1. Export Substack subscribers and import to Kit
2. Set up a redirect or final Substack post pointing people to your Kit-powered newsletter
3. One list, one place, one system

---

### 2. Create a Lead Magnet That Actually Converts

**The problem:** 153 subscribers from 50+ videos means your current email pitch isn't strong enough. "Subscribe to my newsletter" is a weak offer.

**The fix:** Create something specific and valuable enough that people feel like they'd be foolish not to grab it.

**Best option for your audience — "PMP Exam Readiness Quiz":**
- 15-20 questions covering key PMP/RMP knowledge areas
- Instant score + personalized study recommendations
- Email required to get full results + a custom study plan
- Build with Typeform, Tally, or Outgrow (no code needed)
- Quizzes convert at 30-50% — far higher than "subscribe to my newsletter"

**Alternative lead magnets (pick ONE to start):**
- **"The 30-Day PMP Study Plan for Busy Professionals"** — A structured PDF schedule. High perceived value.
- **"50 PMP Questions You'll Probably See"** — Practice questions with explanations. Demonstrates your expertise.
- **"PMP Exam Cheat Sheet"** — One-page reference of key formulas, frameworks, and concepts.

**Action steps:**
1. Build the quiz or create one PDF lead magnet (this weekend)
2. Add it to every YouTube video description
3. Add it as a pop-up or inline CTA on every blog post
4. Pin it on all social profiles
5. Mention it verbally at the end of every new YouTube video

### 3. Add CTAs to All 50+ Existing YouTube Videos

**The problem:** You have a library of 50+ videos that are generating views right now, but probably sending people nowhere.

**The fix:** Retrofit every video with a path to your email list.

**Action steps:**
1. Update ALL video descriptions with a link to your lead magnet at the top (first line, before "In this video...")
2. Add a pinned comment on every video: "Studying for the PMP? Grab my free [lead magnet] → [link]"
3. Add end screens (last 20 seconds) pointing to your lead magnet landing page and your best-performing video
4. Add info cards at the moment you mention something the lead magnet covers

**Expected outcome:** This is the single highest-ROI action you can take. Your 50+ videos are already getting views. Adding CTAs monetizes existing traffic with zero extra content creation.

**Time required:** 2-3 hours to update all 50 descriptions + pinned comments. Do it in one sitting.

---

### 4. Build a Welcome + Nurture Email Sequence in Kit

**The problem:** Getting subscribers means nothing if there's no sequence converting them into course buyers.

**The fix:** Build a 7-email automated sequence that turns new subscribers into students.

**Sequence structure:**

| Email | Day | Purpose | Subject Line Example |
|-------|-----|---------|---------------------|
| 1 | 0 | Deliver lead magnet + introduce yourself | "Your [lead magnet] is here (+ a quick intro)" |
| 2 | 2 | Quick win — one actionable exam tip | "The #1 mistake on PMP risk questions" |
| 3 | 4 | Your story — why you teach this | "Why I became obsessed with project risk" |
| 4 | 7 | Social proof — student success story | "[Student name] passed the PMP in 60 days" |
| 5 | 10 | Overcome the biggest objection | "Think you don't have time to study? Read this." |
| 6 | 13 | Teach a core concept (showcase your style) | "EVM in 5 minutes (the way I wish someone taught me)" |
| 7 | 16 | Direct offer — your course with urgency | "Ready to pass? Here's your plan." |

**Key principles:**
- Every email provides standalone value (not just "buy my course")
- One CTA per email
- Conversational tone — read it out loud, it should sound like you talking
- Email 7 can include a limited-time discount or bonus to create urgency

### 5. SEO-Optimize Your Existing Blog

**The problem:** You have a blog but it may not be targeting the searches your audience actually makes.

**The fix:** Audit what you have and fill gaps with high-intent content.

**High-priority articles to write (or optimize if they exist):**

*Decision-stage (buyers comparing options):*
- "Best PMP Exam Prep Courses [2026] — Honest Comparison"
- "PMI-RMP Exam Prep: Your Options Compared"
- "Is PMP Certification Worth It? (Salary Data + ROI)"

*Awareness-stage (people starting their journey):*
- "How to Pass the PMP Exam (Complete Guide)"
- "PMP vs PMI-RMP: Which Certification Should You Get First?"
- "How Long Does It Take to Study for the PMP?"
- "PMP Exam Study Plan for Busy Professionals"

*Problem-stage (people Google these while studying):*
- "PMP Exam Formulas Cheat Sheet"
- "Understanding Earned Value Management (EVM) for the PMP"
- "Risk Register Template + How to Use It"

**Action steps:**
1. List your existing blog posts and check which keywords they target
2. Identify gaps from the list above
3. Write 1 new article per week
4. Every article includes your lead magnet CTA
5. Embed relevant YouTube videos in each article (this helps both blog SEO and YouTube views)

### 6. Turn YouTube Videos into Blog Posts (Repurposing Flywheel)

**The problem:** You've created 50+ videos worth of content but each video only lives on YouTube. That's single-use content.

**The fix:** Every YouTube video becomes at least 3 more pieces of content.

**The flywheel:**

```
YouTube Video (already exists)
├── Blog post (transcribe + edit into article with keywords)
├── Email to Kit list (key takeaway + link to video)
├── LinkedIn post (one insight from the video)
├── Short-form clip (TikTok/Reels/Shorts — pull the best 60 seconds)
└── Substack→Kit newsletter edition (if transitioning)
```

**Immediate action:** Take your 5 best-performing YouTube videos and turn them into blog posts this month. Use a transcription tool (Descript, Otter.ai, even YouTube's auto-captions) to get the raw text, then edit it into a proper article with keyword targeting.

**Why this matters:** YouTube videos don't rank well in Google. Blog posts do. By publishing written versions, you capture search traffic AND YouTube traffic for the same topic.

### 7. Student Referral Program

**The problem:** Every student who passes their exam tells people about it. Right now there's no system to capture that moment.

**The fix:** Build a simple referral loop triggered by exam success.

**How it works:**
1. When a student reports passing, send an automated congratulations email
2. Include: "Know someone else studying for the PMP? Give them 15% off, and you'll get [reward — Amazon gift card, free coaching session, etc.]"
3. Give them a unique referral link and a pre-written LinkedIn post template ("I just passed my PMP! Here's how I prepared...")
4. Follow up at day 7 and day 30 if they haven't shared

**Pre-written LinkedIn post template to give students:**
> I passed the [PMP/PMI-RMP]! Huge thanks to @44RiskPM for the prep course — the risk management focus was exactly what I needed. If you're studying, I have a 15% discount link: [referral link]

**Expected outcome:** 10-20% of successful students refer at least one person.

### 8. YouTube SEO + Thumbnail Optimization

**The problem:** 50+ videos but flat growth could mean your videos aren't being discovered via YouTube search or suggested videos.

**The fix:** Optimize existing videos for discoverability.

**Title optimization checklist:**
- Does the title include the search term someone would type? ("How to Pass the PMP Exam" > "My PMP Journey Part 3")
- Is it under 60 characters?
- Does it create curiosity or promise a specific outcome?

**Thumbnail optimization checklist:**
- Large, readable text (3-5 words max)
- Your face showing emotion (surprise, excitement, concern)
- High contrast colors
- Consistent branding across videos

**Description optimization:**
- Lead magnet link on line 1
- Full keyword-rich description (200+ words)
- Timestamps for key sections
- Links to related videos and blog posts

**Action step:** Pick your top 10 videos by views and re-optimize titles, thumbnails, and descriptions. This alone can unlock significant additional views from existing content.

### 9. Webinar / Live Study Session (Monthly)

**What:** Host one free live session per month — a study session, Q&A, or exam topic deep-dive.

**Why it fits:** You already create YouTube content, so going live is natural. Live sessions create urgency (must show up) and interaction (Q&A builds trust). They also generate warm leads who've spent 30-60 minutes with you.

**Format ideas:**
- "PMP Exam Crash Course: All 5 Domains in 60 Minutes"
- "Ask Me Anything: PMP Exam Edition"
- "Live Study Session: Risk Management Deep Dive"
- "Study Planning Workshop for Busy Professionals"

**How to start:**
1. Host on YouTube Live (leverages your existing channel and subscribers)
2. Promote to your Kit list + social 1 week before
3. Record and upload as a regular YouTube video after
4. Make a course offer with a time-limited discount at the end

**Expected outcome:** 30-100 live attendees, 5-10% convert to paid when given a compelling offer.

---

### 10. Podcast Guest Tour

**What:** Guest on PM, career development, and professional certification podcasts.

**Why it fits:** You already have the expertise and the content — you just need to get in front of other people's audiences. One 30-minute podcast interview can drive more targeted traffic than weeks of social posting.

**Pitch angles:**
- "How Busy Professionals Can Pass the PMP in 90 Days"
- "Why Risk Management Is the Most Underrated PM Skill"
- "From Aspiring PM to Certified: The Fastest Path"
- "What the PMP Exam Is Really Like (No Sugarcoating)"

**How to start:**
1. List 20 podcasts in PM, career, and professional development
2. Send a short pitch email with your topic, your credibility, and what their audience gets
3. Always mention your lead magnet during the episode
4. Ask the host to link to your lead magnet in show notes
